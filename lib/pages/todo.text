To make your app more exciting and interactive, you could consider adding features like:

1. **Real-Time Notifications**: Notify students about events, classes, or any updates in the area they are located in.

2. **Study Groups and Messaging**: Allow students to form study groups based on their location, send messages, or share resources within the group.

3. **Leaderboard/Challenges**: Create a gamification aspect where students can participate in challenges (like walking to specific areas on campus) and track progress on a leaderboard.

4. **Event Tracker**: Display upcoming campus events, student gatherings, or club meetings, and let users RSVP or check-in when they attend.

5. **Campus Map**: Provide a detailed map of buildings, departments, lecture halls, and amenities like libraries, cafes, etc. with real-time updates (e.g., class schedules, room availability).

6. **Classroom Finder**: Help students navigate to their classrooms based on their schedule. You could integrate a time-table feature where students can see their classes and locations on a map.

7. **Safety Features**: Include a "Safe Walk" option that allows students to share their location with a trusted friend while they walk alone, ensuring they feel safe.

8. **Crowdsourced Heatmaps**: Allow students to submit real-time data, like how crowded certain study areas or campus facilities are, to create a more dynamic heatmap.

9. **Food/Shop Locator**: Add information about food options, student discounts, and store locations, including real-time availability or menu options.

10. **Peer Reviews and Recommendations**: Let students leave reviews about locations, study spots, or campus facilities, helping others find the best places.

These features could not only make your app more functional but also improve the overall student experience, making it more engaging and useful.
